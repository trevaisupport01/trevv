Syllabus Breakdown

7 Core Sessions

Starter covers Sessions 1–4. Professional and VIP cover all 7 sessions, with live instruction and practical exercises.

Get set up on the two most-used AI assistants and finish the class having done real, useful tasks with them.

  • Setting up free ChatGPT and Gemini accounts on your phone and laptop
  • What AI is good at, what it gets wrong, and why you always check its answers
  • First real tasks: rewrite a WhatsApp message, summarise a long document, plan your week
  • Assignment: three everyday tasks done with AI, shown before and after

Move past one-line questions and get the answer you actually want, the first time or after a quick fix.

  • The 4-part prompt: role, task, context and format
  • Giving examples so the AI copies your style
  • Improving a draft step by step instead of accepting the first answer
  • Saving your best prompts as reusable templates
  • Comparing ChatGPT and Claude on the same task
  • Assignment: rewrite three weak prompts and show the improvement

Use AI on the writing and planning you already do at work, school or in your business.

  • Emails, CVs, cover letters, reports and social media posts
  • Changing tone for a boss, a client or an international audience
  • Which tool for which job: a one-page cheat sheet covering ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Grok on X
  • Assignment: one real piece of work you need this week, done with AI

Find information you can rely on, and use AI on your own documents instead of guessing.

  • Using Perplexity for research with sources you can check
  • NotebookLM: upload your own notes or documents and ask questions about them
  • Spotting made-up facts and checking claims against the source
  • Staying safe: what never to paste into AI (passwords, customer data, ID numbers)
  • Starter final project: a researched, fact-checked brief on a topic you choose

Professional and VIP. Turn ideas into flyers, social graphics and short videos without design software.

  • Flyers, social posts and product images with ChatGPT and Gemini image tools, finished in Canva
  • Short videos from an idea: script, visuals and captions using free-tier tools such as Google Veo (in Gemini) and Kling
  • Copyright, using real people's faces, and when to say something was made with AI
  • VIP focus: brand visuals and pitch-deck graphics for your business
  • Assignment: one image post and one 30-second video

Professional and VIP. Go from asking AI questions to having it do repeat work for you.

  • Build your own assistant that remembers your instructions: Gemini Gems hands-on (free), with custom GPTs and Claude Projects demonstrated
  • AI agents that carry out tasks for you, such as ChatGPT agent mode and the Gemini agent, and when to trust them
  • Your first automation with Zapier or Make, for example form entry → spreadsheet → email reply, including Zapier's plain-English agents
  • Where to go next: n8n for bigger workflows
  • VIP focus: automate one real process identified in your automation audit
  • Assignment: one working assistant or automation

Professional and VIP. Show what you built and leave with a workflow you will keep using.

  • Professional: present a working AI workflow that solves a real problem for you
  • VIP: present an AI implementation plan for your business, built with the VIP template suite
  • Feedback from the instructor and your classmates
  • Certificate requirement: attend and complete at least 5 of 7 classes
