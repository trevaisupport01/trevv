For parents and guardians

Safe, simple and useful.

You register your child, you get every message, and your child learns to use AI to understand school work, not to copy it.

Safety

How we keep it safe

Your child is never asked for a password, and we never contact them privately.

  • Parents are the contact. Access codes, class links and updates go to your email and WhatsApp, not to your child.
  • Age-appropriate tools. We only use tools that allow teenagers, and children never paste personal details into AI.
  • Learning, not cheating. We teach children to use AI to understand their work, not to copy homework.
  • Controlled classes. Only registered students are let in to the Google Meet, with the chat and microphones managed by the host.
  • Photos only with consent. We never share your child's name, face or work publicly unless you give permission when registering.
  • No marketing to children. Your child is not added to any mailing list.
What you need

Before the first class

  • A phone, tablet or laptop with internet
  • A Google account for your child (for Gemini), set up with your help
  • About 20 minutes between classes for each short task
  • You nearby for the first class, to help with account setup

Questions

Parents often ask

Who can join?

Children aged 13 to 16 on 14 November 2026. A parent or guardian must register them.

Is it really ₦2,500?

Yes. The Starter package is normally ₦10,000; the Teens Scholarship covers ₦7,500 of it for the first 20 children.

What if all the places are taken?

The registration page shows how many places are left and closes when they are gone. If a payment arrives after the last place is taken, we refund it or offer a place if one opens up.

Does my child need any experience?

No. We start from the beginning. If your child can use a phone and type, they are ready.

Will this help with school work or encourage cheating?

We teach children to use AI to understand topics and revise, and to follow their school's rules. Copying AI answers is not allowed in our classes.

Can I watch the classes?

Yes, you are welcome to sit with your child, especially for the first class.

How does my child get the certificate?

By attending at least 3 of the 4 classes and presenting the final project. We email the certificate to you.
