The four classes

What your child will learn.

Every class is 90 minutes, live on Google Meet, with a short task to try before the next one. All the tools are free.

Checking places…
01Sat 14 Nov · 4:00 PM

Meet AI safely

What AI is and isn't, safe accounts set up with a parent, what never to share, and how to ask good questions.

  • What AI is good and bad at
  • The 4-part prompt: role, task, context, format
  • Checking AI answers against a textbook or teacher

Task: explain one school topic with AI and check one fact.

02Sun 15 Nov · 4:00 PM

AI as a study buddy

Using AI to understand topics and revise, with clear rules for learning without cheating.

  • One topic explained three ways
  • Revision quizzes, flashcards and spotting mistakes
  • A study plan for the week

Task: build a revision quiz or flashcards for a real test.

03Sat 21 Nov · 4:00 PM

Create with AI

Stories, images and a simple presentation in Canva, made the responsible way.

  • Planning a story with AI, then writing it yourself
  • Describing an image: subject, setting, style, colours
  • No real faces, no copied characters, always honest

Task: start the final project with at least two slides.

04Sun 22 Nov · 4:00 PM

Research & showcase

Finding facts with sources and checking them, then every child presents.

  • Checking a fact in four steps
  • A 2–3 minute project: "Explaining something I love"
  • Kind feedback: one star and one wish

Project: present in class and upload the slides.

Certificate

Every child who completes gets a certificate

Attend at least 3 of the 4 classes and present the final project. Each certificate has its own ID that anyone can check on our Verify Certificate page.

Tools used: Gemini and ChatGPT (with a parent's permission), and Canva. A phone or laptop with internet is enough.
